## What I Built Today

### 1. Canonical Candidate Engineering Metrics (SURGE-PY-026)
- Standardized engineering metric extraction across all candidate collector topologies (`app/optimisation/engineering_metrics.py`):
  - **Physical**: Total circuit length ($m$), span count, average span length, span standard deviation.
  - **Spatial**: Parcel intersection area ($m^2$), count of bisected private land parcels, road crossings, high-tension (HT) crossings.
  - **Infrastructure**: Total pole count, terminal pole count, angle pole count, intermediate tangent pole count, junction pole count.
  - **Electrical**: Pandapower AC active losses ($kW$), maximum bus voltage drop ($\Delta V_{\text{max}}$), and cable ampacity loading ($I / I_{\text{thermal}}$).

### 2. Multi-Objective Scoring & Pareto Selection (SURGE-PY-018)
- Built the unified scoring policy engine (`app/scoring/policy.py`):
  - Normalizes metrics into dimensionless $[0, 1]$ utility scores.
  - Groups scores into Physical, Spatial, Infrastructure, and Electrical domains.
  - Applies scenario weight profiles (`BALANCED_DEFAULT`, `CAPEX_MINIMIZED`, `LOSS_MINIMIZED`, `ENVIRONMENTAL_FIRST`).
  - Implements deterministic tie-breaking logic ensuring 100% reproducible candidate selection.

### 3. High-Precision Lifecycle Costing Engine (SURGE-PY-028)
- Implemented arbitrary-precision financial evaluation under `app/costing/` (`lifecycle.py`, `models.py`, `catalogue.py`, `failures.py`):
  - **Conductor CAPEX**: Conductor length $\times$ circuit multiplier $\times$ unit rate per km.
  - **Structural Pole CAPEX**: Itemized count $\times$ unit rate across all four structural classes (`terminal`, `angle`, `intermediate`, `junction`).
  - **Land Acquisition & ROW Compensation**: Fixed fee per parcel plus variable rate based on route overlap length or corridor intersection area.
  - **Operational Losses OPEX (NPV)**: 25-year present value of Pandapower AC active losses based on annual operating hours (8,760 hrs), Loss Load Factor (LLF), energy tariff ($/MWh), and discount rate ($r = 8.0\%$).
  - Implemented arbitrary-precision `Decimal` arithmetic with bankers rounding (`ROUND_HALF_EVEN`) to eliminate floating-point truncation errors in multi-million dollar capital budgets.
