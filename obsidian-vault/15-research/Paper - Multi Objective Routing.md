# Paper Notes — Multi-Objective Routing in Transmission Grids

## Abstract & Key Takeaways
- Explores multi-objective Pareto front generation for transmission line corridor selection.
- Demonstrates combining A* graph search with continuous cost surfaces derived from remote sensing data.
