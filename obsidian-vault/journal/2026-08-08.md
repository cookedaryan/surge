## What I Built Today

### 1. Flyway Database Migration V2 & Spatial JPA Entities
- Implemented `V2__create_optimization_and_gis_tables.sql` in `backend-java/src/main/resources/db/migration/`:
  - `cadastral_parcels`: Table with `geometry(Polygon, 4326)`, GiST spatial index, acquisition cost ($/m²), and unique `(project_id, parcel_id)` constraint.
  - `restricted_areas`: Table with `geometry(Polygon, 4326)`, GiST index, restriction category, and buffer zone distance.
  - `optimization_jobs`: Table tracking job lifecycle status (`PENDING`, `RUNNING`, `COMPLETED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`), objective weights, electrical voltage, max span, and result summaries.
  - `generated_routes`: Table storing `geometry(LineString, 4326)` feeder corridors and `geometry(MultiPoint, 4326)` pole placements with GiST spatial indexes.
- Created Hibernate Spatial JPA domain entities: `CadastralParcel`, `RestrictedArea`, `OptimizationJob`, `GeneratedRoute`, `JobStatus`.

### 2. Enterprise REST APIs & Service Layer (`backend-java`)
- **Asset Service & Controller (`/api/v1/projects/{projectId}`)**:
  - Ingestion of WGS84 GeoJSON `FeatureCollection` payloads.
  - CRUD operations for WTGs and grid Substations.
- **Cadastral & Restricted Area Services (`ParcelService`, `RestrictedAreaService`)**:
  - Ingestion and spatial querying of parcel boundaries and environmental exclusion zones.
- **Optimization Job Orchestrator (`OptimizationJobService`)**:
  - Validates project spatial assets, creates `OptimizationJob` records, and dispatches JSON payload to Python engine via `PythonOptimizationClient` (Spring 6.1 `RestClient`).
  - Handles asynchronous job state transitions and stores computed feeder routes and pole coordinates.
- **Generated Route Service (`RouteService`)**:
  - Parses and persists JTS `LineString` & `MultiPoint` geometries with WGS84 SRID 4326.
  - Computes planar Haversine route lengths.
- **Engineering Report Service (`ReportService`, `ReportController`)**:
  - Calculates network-wide Bill of Materials (BOM): total conductor length, pole counts by structure type, estimated CAPEX, and parcel Right-of-Way compensation.
  - Exports RFC 4180 compliant CSV reports under `/api/v1/projects/{projectId}/reports/bom/csv`.

### 3. Web GIS Map Frontend (`web-map`)
- Built initial Web GIS application in `web-map/` using Vite, HTML5, CSS3, and Leaflet:
  - Custom Leaflet map layers for WTGs, Substations, Feeder Routes, Parcels, and Exclusion Buffers.
  - Drag-and-Drop GeoJSON uploader for instant CAD ingestion.
  - Parameter control panel (Feeder Capacity, Max Span, System Voltage sliders).
  - Live Bill of Materials (BOM) summary card.
  - CSV engineering report download export.
