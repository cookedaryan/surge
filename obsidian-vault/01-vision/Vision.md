# SURGE Vision & Core Capabilities

SURGE is an **AI-powered enterprise platform for renewable-energy collector and evacuation systems**, combining power systems engineering, GIS spatial analysis, mathematical optimization, and artificial intelligence.

The platform designs a complete collector network between multiple Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs) and substations while minimising **total project lifecycle cost**, not merely route distance. It considers feeder configurations, WTG grouping, junctions, pole placement, pole types, variable spans, electrical constraints, accessibility, environmental restrictions, land acquisition, and Right-of-Way (ROW) corridors.

---

## 10 Major Capability Areas

1. **Intelligent Collector-Network Design** — Automatic WTG grouping, feeder assignments, and substation entrance topology.
2. **Multi-Objective Route Optimisation** — Cost, environmental, land impact, and electrical trade-off balancing.
3. **Intelligent Pole Selection** — Dynamic selection of suspension, tension, and terminal pole types.
4. **Variable Span Optimisation** — Terrain-aware span adjustment for sag, elevation, and structural integrity.
5. **ROW Corridor Optimisation** — Cadastral parcel impact minimization and corridor buffer calculations.
6. **Real Cadastral Parcel Intelligence** — Handling irregular parcel boundaries and calculating compensation.
7. **Explainable AI Engineering Decisions** — Transparent route scoring breakdowns and decision rationale.
8. **Scenario Comparison** — Side-by-side comparison of CAPEX, OPEX, and environmental scores.
9. **Cloud-Based Project Management** — Collaborative project, job, and asset management.
10. **Automated Engineering Reports** — One-click generation of BOM, line schedules, and compliance documentation.
