### 3. Canonical Pole Placement & Endpoint Deduplication (SURGE-PY-024 / PY-025)
- **Structural Classification**:
  - `terminal`: Substation take-offs and WTG terminal towers.
  - `angle`: Intermediate poles with line deflection $\ge 5^\circ$.
  - `intermediate` / `tangent`: In-line poles maintaining span constraints ($40\text{ m} \le \Delta s \le 120\text{ m}$).
  - `junction`: Merged convergence points where multiple branch lines meet.
- **Pairwise Spatial Tolerance Deduplication**:
  - Merges shared feeder endpoints and convergence vertices within a strict pairwise spatial tolerance threshold ($2.0\text{ m}$).
  - Prevents transitive over-merging while assigning stable, deterministic junction identifiers (`JUNCTION-001`, `JUNCTION-002`).
  - Separates physical pole counts from individual route span segment counts.

## Architectural Decisions Established

- Keep `/v1` endpoint stable while developing the canonical workflow on `/v2`.
- Deduplicate poles strictly based on topology identity plus projected metric coordinate tolerance; never merge unrelated mid-span line crossings.
- Compute physical pole placement exclusively on the winning candidate topology after multi-objective scoring to avoid wasteful geometry computation on discarded candidates.
