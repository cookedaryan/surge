# Constraints

## Electrical & Physical Constraints
- **C-01 Max Voltage Drop**: Standard feeder maximum voltage drop limit is set to **5%**.
- **C-02 Span Length Limit**: Maximum allowable span between transmission poles is **250 meters** (terrain permitting).
- **C-03 Slope Limit**: Overhead transmission poles cannot be anchored on ground slopes exceeding **30 degrees** without specialized foundation.
- **C-04 Protected Areas**: Hard constraint — 0 meters traversal permitted within restricted forest or sanctuary polygons.
