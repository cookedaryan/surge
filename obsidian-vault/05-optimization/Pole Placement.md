# Variable Span Pole Placement Algorithm

## Objective
Place transmission poles along line corridors while respecting elevation profiles, ground clearances, and maximum span limits.

## Mechanics
- Elevation profiling along route center line.
- Dynamic programming solver for span optimization.
- Structural assignment: Suspension poles for straight runs, Tension/Angle poles at bend points, Terminal poles at substation/WTG connections.
