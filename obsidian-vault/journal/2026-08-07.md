## What I Built Today

### 1. Foundational Python GIS Pipeline (`optimisation-python/app/gis/`)
Implemented the core geographic processing pipeline to ingest raw WGS84 GeoJSON files and reliably convert them into structured, immutable spatial domain models projected into metric UTM coordinates:

1. **Dynamic Coordinate Reference System (CRS) Management (`crs.py`)**:
   - Implemented automated UTM zone detection via `pyproj.database.query_utm_crs_info` based on the project's geographic mean center $(\bar{\lambda}, \bar{\phi})$.
   - Instantiated forward and reverse `pyproj.Transformer` with `always_xy=True` to guarantee robust coordinate order handling.
   - Enables seamless metric transformations between WGS84 (EPSG:4326) and local projected UTM (EPSG:326xx).
2. **Robust Geometry Validation & Repair (`geometry.py` & `geojson.py`)**:
   - Integrated `shapely.validation.make_valid` to automatically repair self-intersecting polygons (e.g., bowtie polygons or inverted boundary rings).
   - Built GeoJSON parser extracting standard Shapely primitives (`Point`, `Polygon`, `LineString`, `MultiPolygon`) with strict schema error reporting.
3. **Spatial Asset Ingestion & Domain Mapping (`preprocessing.py` & `spatial.py`)**:
   - Ingests multiple Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs) and grid Substations.
   - Enforces Point geometry constraints and validates non-negative capacity attributes.
   - Instantiates frozen domain dataclasses: `WindTurbine`, `Substation`, and `ProjectSpatialData`.
