# Project Goals

## Strategic Objectives

1. **Reduce Lifecycle Cost**: Minimize total capital expenditure (CAPEX) + operational expenditure (OPEX) including line losses and land compensation.
2. **Accelerate Engineering Timelines**: Reduce collector network design cycle from weeks to minutes using automated routing and pole placement.
3. **Ensure Electrical & Environmental Compliance**: Guarantee voltage drop compliance, thermal limits, terrain slope safety, and zero restricted-area encroachments.
4. **Provide Full Explainability**: Give power engineers full visibility into why specific routes, pole placements, or conductor types were selected over alternatives.
