It is an **AI-powered enterprise platform for renewable-energy collector and evacuation systems**, combining power systems, GIS, optimisation and artificial intelligence.

The platform must design a complete collector network between multiple Wind Turbine Generators and substations while minimising **total project lifecycle cost**, not merely route distance. It must consider feeder configurations, WTG grouping, junctions, pole placement, pole types, variable spans, electrical constraints, accessibility, environmental restrictions, land acquisition and Right-of-Way corridors.

## Major capabilities required

The problem statement defines ten major capability areas:

1. Intelligent collector-network design
2. Multi-objective route optimisation
3. Intelligent pole selection
4. Variable span optimisation
5. ROW corridor optimisation
6. Real cadastral parcel intelligence
7. Explainable AI engineering decisions
8. Scenario comparison
9. Cloud-based project management
10. Automated engineering reports

These include automatic WTG grouping, feeder assignments, junction placement and deciding how many feeders enter the substation.

The system must also work with irregular parcel polygons, calculate the actual ROW corridor area, estimate compensation and minimise affected parcels rather than treating land as simple rectangular blocks.
