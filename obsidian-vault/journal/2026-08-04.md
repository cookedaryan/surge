## Architectural Decisions Established

- **[[ADR-001 Use FastAPI]]**: Decided to isolate scientific Python libraries behind a typed FastAPI microservice boundary while retaining enterprise persistence in Spring Boot.
- **[[ADR-002 Use PostGIS]]**: Standardized on PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS 3.4 for transactional spatial persistence with WGS84 (EPSG:4326) and GiST indexing.
- **[[ADR-003 ML Ranking]]**: Approved future ML surrogate models for candidate pre-ranking while mandating deterministic engineering solvers as the safety boundary.
- **[[ADR-004 Lifecycle Cost Objective]]**: Established 25-year Net Present Value (NPV) lifecycle cost ($CAPEX + OPEX$) as the primary optimization objective over raw geometric distance.
